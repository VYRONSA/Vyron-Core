## Licence basis

All images were downloaded from Unsplash. For each one, the photo page was
checked on the date obtained and showed exactly: *"Free to use under the
Unsplash License"*. No Unsplash+ (paid) or Getty-supplied images are used.

Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license), as published:

> Unsplash grants you an irrevocable, nonexclusive, worldwide copyright license
> to download, copy, modify, distribute, perform, and use images from Unsplash
> for free, including for commercial purposes, without permission from or
> attributing the photographer or Unsplash.

Restrictions: images cannot be sold without significant modification, and
Unsplash images cannot be compiled to replicate a similar or competing service.
Attribution is not required (credits are kept here for our own records).

### Important: people and brands are NOT covered

Unsplash Terms & Conditions (https://unsplash.com/terms), section 5, state that
*trademarks, logos or brands that appear in images* and *people's images if they
are recognizable* are not included in the Unsplash License, and that *"you may
need the permission of the brand owner … or individual depending on how you use
the Image."*

The copyright licence therefore clearly permits commercial use of these files,
but **no model or property releases come with them**. Using a recognisable
person's image to advertise a product is the kind of use where a release is
normally expected. Before public launch, VYRONSOFT should decide for each image
marked **Approval required** below whether to:

1. accept the risk after its own legal review;
2. obtain a model release from the photographer / subject; or
3. replace it with a licensed image that includes a model release (for example
   from a paid library such as Getty Images, Adobe Stock or Shutterstock).

Images were screened to avoid visible third-party logos and brand names.

## Processing

Images were resized to fit their largest rendered size at 2× pixel density,
re-encoded as progressive JPEG (quality 82), and stripped of EXIF/GPS metadata.
Next.js `<Image>` serves responsive sizes from these files.

The previous placeholders (crops of the design-reference image in
`public/umora/interim/`) have been removed from the project.
